# Singapore itinerary knowledge

## Itinerary planning
Visit Singapore provides itinerary concepts for different trip lengths and traveller interests, including city exploration, family travel, food, shopping, wellness and outdoor experiences.

## Short-trip themes
The official itinerary collection includes short-trip concepts combining cultural experiences, museums, neighbourhood exploration, food and major city attractions. A three-day plan can therefore be structured by themes rather than trying to cover the entire island every day.

## City and culture
The itinerary material highlights areas such as the Civic District, Marina Bay, Chinatown, Little India, Kampong Glam and Orchard Road across different concepts.

## Weather-aware planning
The source collection provides both outdoor and indoor/cultural experiences. This makes it possible to build weather-aware alternatives when the current forecast is supplied separately through MCP.

## Grounding boundary
These pages are planning references, not a source for live weather, current crowding, ticket inventory or live exchange rates.
