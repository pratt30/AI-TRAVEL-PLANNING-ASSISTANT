# Singapore destination knowledge

## Neighbourhoods and cultural areas
Singapore's central areas include Marina Bay, the Civic/Riverside area, Orchard Road, Bugis/Kampong Glam, Chinatown and Little India. Wikivoyage describes Bugis and Kampong Glam as an old Malay district, Chinatown as a Chinese heritage area, and Little India as a major Indian cultural area.

Outside the centre, Sentosa/Harbourfront is a resort-oriented area, East Coast includes beaches and food destinations, and the Mandai area is associated with major wildlife attractions.

## Attractions and experiences
Marina Bay is associated with Gardens by the Bay, Marina Bay Sands, the Singapore Flyer and the Esplanade. Cultural exploration can focus on Chinatown, Little India and Kampong Glam. Sentosa provides resort and family-oriented experiences. East Coast is associated with beaches and food.

## Transportation
Wikivoyage describes Singapore's public transport network as extensive. MRT and LRT form the backbone of rail transport and buses provide broad coverage. Taxis and rideshares are alternatives for destinations that are less convenient by public transport.

## Walking and weather considerations
Singapore is pedestrian-friendly, but tropical heat and humidity can make long outdoor walks tiring. The guide recommends water and notes that rain and thunderstorms can affect outdoor plans.

## Food
Hawker centres are a major part of Singapore's food culture. The guide describes Chinese, Malay, Indian and other culinary influences. Chinatown, Little India and East Coast are discussed in relation to food experiences.
