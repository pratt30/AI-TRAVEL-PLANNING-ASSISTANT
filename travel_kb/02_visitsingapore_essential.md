# Essential Singapore travel information

## Climate
Visit Singapore describes Singapore as having a tropical climate. Current visitor guidance describes temperatures broadly around 25–33°C and recommends light, airy clothing and carrying an umbrella for occasional rainfall. This is general guidance, not a live forecast.

## Language and time
English, Mandarin, Malay and Tamil are Singapore's four official languages. Singapore Standard Time is GMT+8.

## Public transport
Visit Singapore describes the MRT and bus networks as extensive and efficient. It also describes contactless bank cards, mobile wallets, concession cards, EZ-Link cards and the Singapore Tourist Pass as public-transport payment options.

## Accessibility
Visit Singapore states that MRTs and public buses are wheelchair accessible and that open strollers are allowed, with staff assistance available.

## Payments and practical dining
Visit Singapore describes contactless cards and mobile wallets as common payment options. It also advises visitors to return trays and crockery at designated return stations in hawker centres, food courts and coffeeshops.
