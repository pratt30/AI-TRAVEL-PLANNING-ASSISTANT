# Family travel

## Family-friendly activities
Visit Singapore's family itinerary material highlights National Gallery, ArtScience Museum, Singapore River experiences, Sentosa, Universal Studios Singapore, Bouncetopia, Skyline Luge and Singapore Botanic Gardens.

## Indoor options
ArtScience Museum and National Gallery are examples of indoor/cultural activities described in the family-oriented material. The material also describes interactive experiences as suitable for children.

## Outdoor options
Singapore Botanic Gardens and Sentosa are examples of outdoor-oriented family experiences.

## Planning for families
A family itinerary can mix cultural/indoor activities with outdoor activities and use weather information to change the balance.

## Grounding boundary
The source does not establish current opening status, ticket availability or live weather. Do not invent these.
